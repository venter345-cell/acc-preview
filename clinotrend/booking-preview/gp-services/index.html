General practice, Midrand

GP consultations at Grand Central Airport, Midrand

General practice (GP) consultations with Dr A.O. Ndhlovu for acute illness, chronic conditions and health screening, for people who live or work nearby and for flight crew.

Medical aid and payment

What the GP can help with

  • Consultations for acute illness in adults and children
  • Chronic condition care, for example diabetes, high blood pressure (hypertension), asthma and HIV
  • Blood tests, ECG and general health screening
  • Dispensing, and physical wellness checks for flight crew
  • Referrals to specialists when needed

Not for emergencies

Online booking is not for emergencies. In an emergency call 10177, or 112 from a mobile phone, or go to your nearest emergency unit.
