What we found

The service is strong. Most of it just isn't reaching pilots online yet.

We looked at Clinotrend the way a pilot would on 19 October 2026: your website, the searches pilots type, map listings, AI assistants and a recent patient's booking experience. These are the five things that would make the biggest difference.

01

Phone bookings go quiet after the call

A recent patient found you online, phoned and booked. After that there was no confirmation, reminder, directions or what-to-bring note. On a phone screen, the online booking button sits near the bottom of the first screen.

Fix: confirmations and reminders for every booking, and online booking as the obvious first step.
02

The tracking tag is a placeholder

The website's Google Ads tag uses the ID "AW-CLINOTREND", which looks like a placeholder that was never replaced. There is no analytics or call tracking yet, so no booking or call could be counted.

Fix: a real tag, Google Analytics, and tracking for bookings, calls and WhatsApp taps.
03

No public reviews yet

The listings we found for the practice show no ratings so far. Nearby examiners do have reviews, and pilots often read them when choosing.

Fix: after each visit, a simple message asking patients for an honest Google review.
04

AI tools can't read most of the site

The website's words only appear after the browser runs its code. The crawlers behind ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity don't run that code, so they see little more than the title. You appear for Midrand searches, mostly through the SACAA list, but not for wider Gauteng searches.

Fix: a dedicated aviation medicals page with text that every crawler can read.
05

Small details that affect trust and sharing

The page mentions transparent pricing but shows no prices, there are no SACAA forms to download, the class wording differs from SACAA's own descriptions, opening hours differ between listings, and the link preview image doesn't show on WhatsApp, LinkedIn or Facebook.

Fix: tidy each of these with you, starting in stage one.
