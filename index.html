Example: an aviation medical examiner practice

For aviation medical examiners: more pilots booked, fewer empty slots, every renewal captured.

Aviation medicals are a renewal business. Every pilot, controller and cabin crew member must come back on a fixed cycle. Yet booking often means downloading forms, emailing reception and waiting for a callback in office hours. Here is how we would run growth for an example practice near an airfield.

Before the booking

Found for the exact search

Pages and ads for each search pilots really use: "aviation medical examiner near me", "class 1 medical" plus your city, "class 2 medical", "pilot medical renewal", "cabin crew medical". Your Google profile, aviation directories and AI answers all say the same, clear thing.

At the booking

Booked in minutes, not days

Pilots choose their class, see what is included and what to bring, pick a slot and upload their forms in one go. Calls you miss get a text back with the booking link. Questions after hours get an instant answer.

After the visit

Renewals on autopilot

Each pilot gets a reminder well before their medical expires, with a one tap rebooking link. Happy pilots are asked for a review, and asked to pass your name to their flight school or crew room.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

Do I have to change my booking system or software?

No. We work with what you already use where we can. If a tool is holding you back, we will explain why and suggest a better option, but the choice is yours.

Do you guarantee a number of new patients or clients?

No, and be careful of anyone who does. We agree clear goals with you, build the full system, and show you exactly what is working so you can judge for yourself.

What is AEO and GEO, and do I need it?

AEO and GEO mean making your business easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI answers to understand and recommend. More people now ask an AI who to see. Those answers lean on your website, your Google profile, your listings and your reviews, so we tidy up all of them.

How do you handle patient privacy and medical advertising rules?

Clinical records stay in your clinical systems. Marketing tools only hold contact and booking details, with consent. We keep ads and pages factual and within the rules of your professional body, and you approve every message before it goes out.

Who owns the website, ad accounts and data?

You do. Accounts are set up in your name, and we work inside them. If you ever leave, everything stays with you.

How much of my time does this take?

A short kickoff call, quick approvals on key messages, and short check ins. We do the building, writing and setup.

Is this only for medical practices?

No. The same system works for any business that lives on enquiries and bookings, from clinics to energy, roofing and software companies. The aviation medical example shows how it fits one type of practice.
