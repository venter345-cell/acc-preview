SEO keyword changes, for review

Changes so the site can show up for the searches people actually use: a tighter aviation medicals title, a new near-you section, a homepage heading that leads with "aviation medical examiner", a new Class 4 medical page, and (added 17 November) an Aviation medicals dropdown in the main menu with a dedicated cabin crew medical page. Preview only: nothing here is on the live site.

How to review and comment

  1. Use the side by side views below on a computer. Desktop shows pages at full width. Phone shows each at phone width (390 px). On a phone, the two pages stack, or you can open each page on its own.
  2. To leave a note on a new page, open it, tap the dark Add comment button at the bottom left, tap the exact spot your note is about, type it and tap Save comment. Add your name the first time. Comments also work inside the phone frame.
  3. The yellow box on this review page, marked [RUBEN TO CONFIRM], lists choices for you. Every regulatory fact on the new pages has been checked against the Civil Aviation Regulations (Parts 64 and 67); anything that could not be confirmed was left off the pages.

This preview is noindex. The live site and cPanel have not been changed. Bookings from this preview are disabled. The Class 4 Book button still goes to the Class IV calendar path, /book/?service=class-4.

What changed

  • Aviation medicals page title. The requested title was 67 characters, which is over the usual 60 character limit, so "| ClinoTrend" was dropped. The title is now "Aviation Medical Examiner and Aviation Doctor, Midrand" (54 characters), which keeps both search phrases. The meta description now includes aviation medical examiner, Midrand, Johannesburg, Class II and Class IV, and booking.
  • New section on /aviation-medicals/: "Looking for an aviation doctor near you?" It names Johannesburg, Pretoria, Centurion, Sandton and Kempton Park, says the clinic is in Midrand and within easy reach, and gives usual routes in general terms (N1, Gautrain, R21, R101). No journey times are stated. The copy also uses aero medical examiner, flight medical examiner and DAME. When the 14 local pages go live, this section links to them.
  • Homepage H1: "Aviation medical examiner for pilots and cabin crew in Midrand" (was "Aviation medicals for pilots and cabin crew"). The Class IV card now points to the new Class 4 page.
  • Main menu (new, 17 Nov): "Aviation medicals" is now a dropdown: Aviation medicals overview, Class 2 medical, Class 4 medical, Cabin crew medical, Renewals, Recreational pilots. It opens on hover or with the arrow button, works with the keyboard (Enter, Escape, arrow down), and on phones the arrow under the menu expands the sub items. The separate top level "Class 4 medical" item has moved into the dropdown.
  • New /cabin-crew-medical/ page (new, 17 Nov) for working cabin crew and for aspiring flight attendants and cabin crew trainees: who needs it (regulation 64.01.6 covers applicants as well as licence holders), Class 4 as the standard (67.00.2), Class 2 if the airline or operator asks, what to expect, what to bring (67.00.7), validity (67.00.6(4)), the yearly declaration (67.00.9(3)), renewals and the 30 day extension (67.00.6(6)), location and routes, from R1,500, Book (/book/?service=class-4), 011 568 7979, and questions with FAQ schema. It replaces the approved but unpublished /aviation-medicals/class-4-cabin-crew/ page, whose content is folded in; the old address now forwards to the new page and should get a 301 at go-live. The homepage, aviation medicals page, Class 4 page, cabin crew guide, footer and local pages link to it.
  • New /class-4-medical/ page in the main menu and sitemap: Class 4 aviation medical certificate for cabin crew and recreational pilots. Validity and the yearly declaration follow Part 67 (67.00.6 and 67.00.9). The doctor issues Class 2 and Class 4, not Class 1. The fee is from R1,500. The Book button goes to /book/?service=class-4. The cabin crew guide and the aviation medicals page link here.

[RUBEN TO CONFIRM]

These are choices for you. The facts on the pages were checked against the regulations and are not listed here.

  1. Journey times. The near-you and cabin crew location sections do not publish drive times. They use general routes only. Say if you want approximate times added, clearly labelled as approximate.
  2. Aviation medicals title. The preview uses "Aviation Medical Examiner and Aviation Doctor, Midrand" (54 characters). The alternative with the brand is "Aviation Medical Examiner and Doctor, Midrand | ClinoTrend" (58 characters).
  3. Menu labels. The dropdown uses "Class 2 medical" and "Class 4 medical"; some older wording on the site still says Class II and Class IV. Say if you want one style everywhere.

Wording change from the last preview: the Class 4 page no longer says "Class 5 is not a self declaration". Regulation 67.00.2(8) calls it a "Class 5 medical self-declaration" signed by a registered medical practitioner, so the page now says the Class 5 declaration has to be signed by a medical practitioner registered under the Health Professions Act, which matches 67.00.2(8) and (9).
