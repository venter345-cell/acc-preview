What the new page covers

  • Hero: occupational health medicals for Midrand and Gauteng employers, with Dr A.O. Ndhlovu (DOHM, University of Pretoria). Book and phone or WhatsApp.
  • Services: pre-employment, periodic and exit medicals; PrDP medicals; workplace health risk assessments; incapacity consultations; audiometry and spirometry; wellness days and employee health programmes.
  • Who it is for: commercial and industrial employers, employees sent by their employer, and professional drivers who need a PrDP medical. Areas served: Midrand, Johannesburg, Pretoria, Centurion, Randburg, Kempton Park and Germiston.
  • What to bring: ID or passport, employer forms, glasses or contact lenses, and a list of medicines. Corporate quote and group bookings for employers.
  • Location: Grand Central Airport, Midrand, with the practice address, hours, directions and a map. By appointment only.
  • Questions and booking: what to bring, how fees work (quote only), then a closing booking call to action.
