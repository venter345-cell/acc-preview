Fun 404 page, for review

A new "Not cleared for takeoff" page for addresses that do not exist, with a hand-drawn plane, helicopter and gyrocopter. Preview only: nothing here is on the live site.

How to review and comment

  1. Open the 404 page on a computer, or use the phone view link to see it at 390 px wide.
  2. To leave a note, open the page, tap the dark Add comment button at the bottom left, tap the exact spot your note is about, type it and tap Save comment. Add your name the first time. Comments also work inside the phone frame.
  3. The aircraft move a little (the helicopter rotor spins, the plane flies past, the gyrocopter bobs). If your device asks to reduce motion, they stay still.

This preview is noindex. The live site, cPanel and DNS have not been changed. On the live site, any unknown address already returns a real HTTP 404 and would show this page after go-live. On this preview host, open the 404 page directly (a made-up address under the preview cannot use the live server rule).

What is on the page

  • A large 404, with a helicopter in the 0, a plane flying past, and a gyrocopter to the side. All three are inline drawings, not photographs or stock art.
  • The heading "Not cleared for takeoff", and the line "This page doesn't exist, or it's been moved to a new runway."
  • A pill button, Book your medical, which goes to the booking page.
  • Small links to Aviation medicals, Class 2, Class 4, Cabin crew, Flight schools and Contact.
  • The usual white-background logo, header and footer.
