What we found

Eight things worth fixing, with the evidence.

Everything here was checked on 23 November 2026 from public data, Google's own tools and the WhatsApp widget we run for you.

F1Leads

WhatsApp leads arrive with almost no detail

Before a chat starts, the widget asks one thing: business name. Odoo gets a name and a page link, often with no phone, email or service attached, so every lead looks the same and none are scored. Your team has to ask the basics by hand, one chat at a time, and there is no easy way to see which chats turned into quotes or sales.

About 1 in 20 business names entered is one or two characters long. Field names have changed over time (Full Name, Business Name, WhatsApp Number), so the data in Odoo is not consistent.
Ask 4 short questions in WhatsApp, score the answer, fill proper Odoo fields and link every lead to its quote and sale.
F2Leads

A big share of chats come from outside the UAE

More than 1 in 4 WhatsApp chats in the last year came from outside the UAE, mostly Bangladesh, India and Pakistan. Some will be real, most are unlikely to book a Dubai shoot.

In November so far, about 1 in 5 chats that came from a Google ad was from outside the UAE.
Check ad location settings, add a polite location question and route out-of-market chats away from the sales queue.
F3Measurement

No analytics or ad tracking on the website

When we load your homepage, no Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads tag or Meta pixel fires. Google Ads is running, but it can't see which clicks become chats or shoots, so it can't learn what to buy more of.

Google's Ads Transparency Center (checked 23 November 2026) shows Shapes Defined Photographic Trading LLC as a verified advertiser with 29 ads in text, image and video, still running this week, with lines like "360 Product Photography - Quick 24-Hour Delivery" and "Viral Content Video Studio". You pay for clicks every day, but with no conversion tag or GA4 on the site, that spend can't be tied to chats or bookings. Ad links also pass the text "{campaignname}" instead of the real campaign name, so campaign names never reach your CRM.
Install GA4 and Ads conversion tracking, fix the tracking template, then send qualified leads and won deals from Odoo back to Google Ads. A click on a WhatsApp button is not a lead, and only a share of clicks become real chats, so ads should learn from chats, quotes and sales instead.
F4Speed

The homepage is heavy on mobile

Google PageSpeed scores the homepage 58 on mobile and 60 on desktop. The largest image takes 21.2 seconds to appear on a mobile test, and the page weighs about 8.7 MB, most of it images.

Accessibility 91, Best Practices 96, SEO 83. SEO flags: no meta description, images without alt text.
Resize and compress the hero and logo images, lazy load below the fold. Same look, much faster.
F5Search

Old pages that brought leads now go nowhere

Your previous site had pages for jewellery, fashion, ghost mannequin, 360 video, model shoots, prices and get a quote. We spot checked 25 of those old addresses: 23 now show "page not found".

People were still landing on those old pages and starting WhatsApp chats until late October.
Redirect every old address to the closest new page, so Google and returning buyers land in the right place.
F6Search

Google and AI get very little to read

The homepage title is just "Shapes Defined". No page has a meta description, there is no business or service markup (schema), and about 4 in 5 homepage images have no alt text. The sitemap lists 9 pages.

No FAQ page, no pricing guide, no industry pages, no blog.
Write titles and descriptions, add business, service and FAQ markup, and build the missing pages.
F7Reviews

Great rating, few reviews

4.8 stars is excellent. With 46 reviews, though, you sit behind studios like Mirrorless (239) and SkyLight (312). Review count and freshness are one of the things Google and AI answers lean on for "best studio" questions.

Your reviews name Muna, Chris and the team by name. That is gold for trust.
Ask every happy client for a review on WhatsApp after delivery, with a direct link.
F8Social

Your channels undersell your work

LinkedIn has 823 followers and an About section that still describes an older business. The Facebook page linked from your site has 64 followers, and your Google listing points to a different Facebook page. Phone numbers differ across the site, the widget and listings.

You produce content for big brands every day. Very little of it shows up on your own channels.
Refresh profiles, pick one page per platform, and post your own work weekly with AI agents doing the heavy lifting.
