How to review and comment

  1. Open an article. Read on desktop shows it as it will look on a computer. View as phone shows it at phone size. You can also open this page on your phone.
  2. To leave a note, tap the dark Add comment button (speech bubble) at the bottom left of the article, then tap the exact spot your note is about, type it and tap Save comment. For a note on the whole article, use General comment. Add your name the first time.
  3. Every article lists its official sources (SACAA and the Civil Aviation Regulations, Part 67) at the bottom.
