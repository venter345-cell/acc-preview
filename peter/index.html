The commercials

A small monthly fee, and a share when we win together.

Proposed, to confirm together Win share: 5% of invoice value
  • ACC earns 5% of the invoice value, excluding VAT, on every job or tender ACC brings in
  • Paid within 7 days of the client paying Thandolufuno
  • Applies to repeat work from that client for 24 months
  • No share on your own existing clients
  • If the client doesn't pay, nothing is due to ACC

Example: if ACC brings in a R200,000 job, ACC's share is R10,000.
