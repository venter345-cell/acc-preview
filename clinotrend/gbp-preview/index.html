4 · Questions people ask

11 answers that feed Google's "Ask about this place"

Google stopped new Q&A on profiles in late 2025. Its AI now answers from the profile, the website and reviews. These answers go on the website FAQ page (already built in the site preview), so Google, ChatGPT and other AI tools quote the same facts.

Do you do medicals for pilots and cabin crew?

Yes. Dr A.O. Ndhlovu is a SACAA Regular Designated Aviation Medical Examiner (MR 336) and does SACAA Class II and Class IV medicals for pilots and cabin crew at Grand Central Airport, Midrand.

Which class do cabin crew need?

Cabin crew need a SACAA Class IV medical certificate (SACAA Part 67). Dr Ndhlovu examines cabin crew for initial and renewal Class IV medicals. (SACAA's aviation medicine page still lists cabin crew under Class II: to confirm with Dr Ndhlovu.)

Who needs a Class IV medical?

Cabin crew, and recreational pilots, for example microlight, glider, gyroplane and free balloon pilots.

Can I get a Class 1 or Class 3 medical here?

No. Class I (airline transport and commercial pilots) and Class III (air traffic controllers) medicals can only be done by a SACAA Senior DAME.

What should I bring?

Your ID or passport, your aviation licence, your most recent medical certificate, your logbook if renewing, your medical aid card if applicable, a list of medicines, and the glasses, contact lenses or hearing aids you use. Bring recent specialist reports if you have a medical condition.

Which tests are done on the day?

A resting ECG, vision testing, a hearing test (audiometry) and urine screening are done on site, with your medical history and a physical examination.

Can I get my aviation medical done on the same day?

Most aviation medicals are completed on the same day, depending on your case. If you meet the medical standards and no further reports are needed, the examiner can issue your certificate at the end of the examination. Medicals are by appointment only, so please book first.

How can I pay?

Payment by EFT or card is required at the time of your visit. We accept most medical aids and charge medical aid rates. Self-pay patients receive a quote before treatment.

How long is the certificate valid?

According to SACAA: Class 2 is valid for 24 months under 40 and 12 months from 40. Class 4 is valid for 60 months under 40 and 36 months from 40. Your examiner confirms what applies to you.

How long does the medical take?

Allow about 30 to 60 minutes. Please arrive 10 minutes early to complete paperwork.

How do I find the practice?

Enter Grand Central Airport at the main gate and go in through the main entrance of the airport building. Take the first right once inside, and Clinotrend is straight ahead.

Can I fly in?

Yes. Grand Central Airport is FAGC. The apron and visitor aircraft parking are directly outside the Gate 2 terminal doors.

Can I walk in?

No. The practice works by appointment only, because each medical is prepared before your visit. Book online, call 011 568 7979 or WhatsApp 078 255 1405.

Do you take group bookings?

Yes. Group bookings for flight schools, cabin crew academies, airlines and employers are arranged in advance. Call 011 568 7979 or email info@clinotrend-health.co.za.

Do you do visa medicals?

Yes, by appointment. Bring the medical form from your embassy or immigration authority and your passport. Some countries only accept their own panel physicians, so check your visa instructions first.

How much does it cost?

Fees are given when you book. Call 011 568 7979 or WhatsApp 078 255 1405. (Fee to confirm with Dr Ndhlovu before any price is published.)

Honest reviews, asked for after every visit

Proposal: "An automated message after each visit asking patients for an honest Google review. A direct review link, nothing scripted or rewarded. Help replying to reviews." Runs in GoHighLevel once the calendar is live.

Direct review linkOne tap to Google's review form. QR card at reception for people who prefer it.
One gentle reminderAfter 5 days if no review, then stop. Never more than one reminder.
Reply and reportACC drafts a reply within 1 working day for approval. Monthly report: requests sent, reviews received.

The message (WhatsApp or SMS)

Hi [first name], thank you for visiting ClinoTrend Health & Aviation today. If you have a moment, an honest Google review helps other pilots and cabin crew find the practice: [review link] Reply STOP to opt out.

No mention of the reason for the visit or any result. Sent to every attended booking, not only happy ones.

HPCSA and Google rules we follow

  • No incentives, discounts or draws for reviews.
  • No scripted wording and no asking for 5 stars.
  • Ask everyone, not only happy patients (no review gating).
  • Replies never confirm someone was a patient or mention health details (HPCSA confidentiality, POPIA).
  • Reviews are not reused as testimonials on the website or in ads (HPCSA Booklet 2, Rule 3).
  • Staff and family do not review the practice.
