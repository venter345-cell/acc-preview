Website

  • Full rebuild of the old one-page site into prerendered pages, with wording checked against HPCSA rules. Aviation fees are shown as from R1,500.
  • New service pages: aviation medicals, Class 4, cabin crew, occupational health, GP services, flight schools (plus a student handout), the online SACAA application form, a review page, and a real 404 page.
  • 14 local and service pages under aviation medicals (Gauteng, Midrand, Johannesburg, Pretoria, Centurion, East Rand, Lanseria, fly-in, Class 2, renewal, student pilot, recreational pilots, cost, and a local index).
  • 17 articles were already live before today. Today's two additions are "Can you be a pilot with asthma" (with the in-practice lung function test) and "Pilot hearing test: what the SACAA medical checks".
  • Visual redesign (18 Nov), speed work, and search titles and text aimed at the phrases people actually use.

Practice systems

  • aCc set up for the practice: contacts, pipelines, and four public calendars (Class II first medical, Class II or IV renewal, Class IV, and GP or occupational).
  • Online booking on the website, plus the ability for reception to add a booking after a call.
  • Appointment confirmation, 24 hour and 2 hour reminders, cancellation handling, a new-lead alert, and a post-visit review request by email.
  • Appointment emails are sent from info@clinotrend-health.co.za (branded sending domain live 5 Nov).
  • The one booking from the old system was moved into aCc on 28 Oct.
  • Google Business Profile basics were filled in on 26 Oct (description, hours, WhatsApp, 11 services, photos). Google posts have been published. The listing is not yet verified, so Google review requests are still held.

Tracking and ads
