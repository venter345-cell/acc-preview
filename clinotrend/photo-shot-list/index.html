Phone photo shot list

For Dr Ndhlovu or the practice manager. About 20 minutes with a phone, for the Google Business Profile and the website. Prepared by Ads Creation Centre, 26 October 2026.

Before you start: privacy
  • No patients in any photo, not even in the background.
  • No patient names, files, forms, appointment books or computer screens in view. Turn screens off or face them away.
  • Staff only appear if they agree to it.
  • No filters, no editing beyond straightening and cropping.
Phone settings
  • Clean the lens. Use the normal 1x camera, not the wide angle or portrait mode.
  • Hold the phone sideways (landscape) unless the shot says otherwise.
  • Switch on the room lights and open the blinds. No flash.
  • Take 3 or 4 of each shot, then send the best ones on WhatsApp as a document (not as a photo) so the quality stays full size, or share them in a Google Drive folder.
