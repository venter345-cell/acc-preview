Visual redesign, for review

A new look for the ClinoTrend site: a full-width homepage hero, refined type and spacing, a clearer menu and booking bar, and calmer motion. All page copy, titles, fees and URLs are unchanged. Preview only: nothing here is on the live site.

How to review and comment

  1. Open a page on a computer, or use the phone view link to see it at 390 px wide.
  2. To leave a note, open the page, tap the dark Add comment button at the bottom left, tap the exact spot your note is about, type it and tap Save comment. Add your name the first time. Comments also work inside the phone frame.
  3. The homepage uses a new photograph of a pilot and cabin crew member. It is an illustrative image, not a photo of ClinoTrend staff or patients. Other pages use a refined gradient for now, until further photographs are approved.

This preview is noindex. The live site, cPanel and the held go-live package have not been changed. Bookings from this preview are disabled.

What changed in the look

  • Homepage hero is a full-width photograph with a dark overlay so the existing heading, copy and buttons stay readable.
  • A factual trust strip (SACAA designated AME, HPCSA registered, Grand Central Airport, from R1,500).
  • Refined header, Aviation medicals dropdown, footer (white-background logo), cards, FAQ accordions and section spacing.
  • Sticky mobile bar: Book, Call and WhatsApp (WhatsApp still opens the existing chat widget).
  • Subtle scroll reveals, a slow hero zoom, and hover states. These stay still if the browser asks for reduced motion.
