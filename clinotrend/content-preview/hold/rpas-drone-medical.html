Not every drone operation in South Africa needs a full aviation medical. Some remote-pilot work is done on a self-declaration. Other operations, including some beyond-visual-line-of-sight work and higher RPAS classes, have been described in SACAA Part 101 material as needing a Class 4 medical. Confirm the current rule for your licence before you book. ClinoTrend can do Class 4 medicals if that is what you need.
