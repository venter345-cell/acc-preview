How we work with flight schools

The student pathway

Part 61, regulation 61.02.1, says an applicant for a student pilot licence (SPL) or integrated student pilot licence (ISPL) must be 15 years or older, hold a valid medical certificate issued in terms of Part 67, and be registered with an approved training organisation (ATO). An SPL is what allows a student to fly solo under instruction, so the medical comes first. See the text of regulation 61.02.1.

Regulation 67.00.2 lists the SPL for aeroplane, airship or helicopter under Class 2, alongside the private pilot licence (PPL). Recreational student licences, such as a microlight or glider student licence, sit under Class 4. Confirm the class your course requires. The classes are set out in the amended Part 67 classes of medical certificates and on the SACAA aviation medicine page.

A commercial pilot licence, an airline transport pilot licence and an instrument rating need a Class 1 medical, which only a SACAA Senior DAME may issue. Dr A.O. Ndhlovu is a Regular DAME (MR 336). ClinoTrend does not currently issue Class 1.

What students should bring

  • A South African ID or a passport
  • A list of every medicine they take
  • Glasses or contact lenses, if they use them
  • Any reports from their own doctor if they have a medical condition
  • The SACAA application completed beforehand. The Aviation Medical Report form, CA 67-02(a), is completed on the day.

Students can start from 15. If a student is under 18, contact the practice to confirm any extra requirements before you book. The CA 67-02(a) form is on the SACAA site.

Turnaround

By appointment only. Each medical is prepared before your visit, so please book first. Most aviation medicals are completed on the same day, depending on your case. Group bookings for flight schools, cabin crew academies, airlines and employers are arranged in advance. Book a few weeks before a student expects to fly solo, so there is time if the doctor asks for a further test or a report.

At ClinoTrend a resting ECG, vision testing, a hearing test (audiometry) and urine screening are done on site. The doctor tells the student if a chest X-ray or any further test or report is needed.

Chest X-ray for an initial medical

SACAA medical requirements list a chest X-ray at the initial examination, and afterwards only when it is indicated on clinical grounds. A chest X-ray is not done at the practice. If the doctor refers a student, there are independent radiology practices nearby where a chest X-ray can be conducted. Phone ahead to confirm an appointment. ClinoTrend does not operate these practices and does not receive a fee from them.

Questions flight schools ask

Can students come in as a group on one day?

+

Yes. A school can book a group of students for the same day, arranged in advance. Medicals are by appointment only. Use the form on this page, call 011 568 7979, or WhatsApp 078 255 1405 with the number of students and the weeks that suit you. How many we can see on one day depends on the diary at the time.

How far ahead should a school book?

+

For a group, get in touch a few weeks ahead so we can set aside the times you need. For a first medical, booking a few weeks before a student expects to fly solo also leaves time if the doctor asks for a chest X-ray or a further report.

Can the school get confirmation that a medical was done?

+

We can confirm to the school that a named student attended and that a medical examination was completed, only if that student has given consent for us to tell the school. We do not share medical findings, test results or certificate details with the school. This follows POPIA and the confidentiality of a medical consultation. Students can also show the school their certificate themselves.

Do you issue Class 1 medicals?

+

No. Dr A.O. Ndhlovu is a SACAA Regular Designated Aviation Medical Examiner (MR 336) and issues Class 2 and Class 4 medical certificates. ClinoTrend does not currently issue Class 1. A Class 1 medical, which is required for a commercial pilot licence, an airline transport pilot licence and an instrument rating, must be done by a SACAA Senior DAME.

How much does an aviation medical cost?

+

Aviation medicals start from R1,500. Extra tests or reports are added only if the doctor finds they are needed during the medical. We confirm the fee when you book.

More detail for students is on the student pilot medical guide and the aviation medicals page.
