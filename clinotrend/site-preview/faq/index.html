Questions and answers

How do I book an appointment?

+

Use the online booking form on this website, call 011 568 7979 or send a WhatsApp to 078 255 1405. An online booking is a request: we email you when it is received and again when it is confirmed.

How do I change or cancel my booking?

+

Use "View Booking" on this website with the email and phone number you booked with, or call 011 568 7979. Please give at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee.

Do you accept medical aid?

+

Medical aid cover differs between services and plans, so please check with us before your visit and bring your medical aid card. Self-pay patients receive a quote before treatment.

What should I bring to any appointment?

+

Your ID or passport, your medical aid card if applicable, and a list of the medicines you take. Aviation medicals need more documents: see the checklist on the aviation medicals page.

How is my medical information handled?

+

Your medical information is confidential and handled under POPIA. The privacy notice on this website explains how.
