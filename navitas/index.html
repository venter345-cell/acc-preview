1. What we understand

How Murdoch Dubai and Navitas grow campus revenue.

Murdoch University Dubai is operated in Dubai Knowledge Park (Block 18, expanding toward 4,000 sq m in 2026) by Navitas Middle East FZ-LLC under a 15-year agreement with Murdoch University Perth (2019 to 2033). Navitas runs campus operations, staffing, marketing and recruitment while Murdoch holds academic control and awards the degrees.

Tuition & intakes

Three intakes across pathway and degree tiers

Core revenue is student tuition across January, May and September intakes: Foundation (AED 47,820/year), Diplomas (AED 61,160), Undergraduate degrees in Business, IT, Communication and Psychology (AED 61,160/year; AED 183,480 full 24 units incl 5% VAT), plus MBA and MEd programmes, net of scholarships.

Agent machine

High-volume global education agent channel

Across Navitas globally, roughly 2,000 contracted agents across 125 countries drive around 75% of international student placements, earning commission on enrolment. Murdoch Dubai lists a Find an Agent finder and Agents' Portal directly in its Apply flow.

Direct UAE & parent growth

Adding direct enrolments beside agents

UAE-resident families, international school students and direct digital enquirers carry no agent commission and often decide as a household. Strengthening direct enquiry handling and parent follow-up grows campus margin without competing with the agent network.

2. Questions to start with

Before touching ads or copy, how does a contact move today?

From your public site we can already see Navitas Marketo (007-GQS-374) and the StudyLink application portal. So the conversation is not "do you have tools", it is how each opportunity is captured, documented and routed.

Which CRM is the system of record?

Do enquiries live in Marketo alone, sync into Salesforce or another CRM, or sit split between Marketo and StudyLink? Who owns changes to fields and templates at campus vs group level?

How is each opportunity documented?

Can your team record number of students in a household, student age or school grade, curriculum, programme interest, intake, and parent vs student vs agent so contacts enter the right marketing flow?

What triggers automated nurture?

When a Contact Us, Brochure, Open Day, or started-but-unsubmitted StudyLink application arrives, which fields must be present before an email or WhatsApp sequence can run?

Who follows up direct leads, and how fast?

When a prospect checks WhatsApp consent on your Contact form, who messages them, on what target response time, and are parent enquiries handled differently from student enquiries?

Is the brief Murdoch Dubai only?

Is our scope strictly the Murdoch University Dubai direct funnel (UAE, GCC and inbound digital), rather than regional recruitment into Navitas colleges elsewhere?

How are direct vs agent overlaps tracked?

Can you see cleanly from enquiry to enrolled student what each paid search, Meta, school, and Open Day channel produces alongside agent-referred applications?

UOWD & Curtin: closest Australian rivals

Where they win today
UOWD shows up first alongside Birmingham in "best for business" AI search results, runs live chat, and delivered a personal-style advisor email in 14 minutes. Curtin pairs WhatsApp Joinchat with required Student/Parent/Agent routing on Open Days and full Meta/TikTok/LinkedIn pixels.
Where Murdoch beats them
Murdoch's mobile homepage (2.0 s LCP) is far faster than UOWD (8.4 s) and Curtin (38.3 s), and sits right inside Dubai Knowledge Park rather than DIAC.

Middlesex & De Montfort: mid-band UK neighbours

Where they win today
Middlesex removes friction on campus visits with a live Jotform tour calendar and Request a Call toolbar, and advertises 127 nationalities. De Montfort routes prospective families straight to regional WhatsApp coordinators.
Where Murdoch matches or leads
Middlesex's enquiry form showed the same silent-confirmation blind spot as Murdoch in our test, and De Montfort sits outside Knowledge Park without a published public enquiry form.

Heriot-Watt & Birmingham: upper tier

Why they matter differently
Heriot-Watt sits ~29% above Murdoch (AED 78,650) with a stronger STEM identity, but competes in the same DKP park. University of Birmingham Dubai (AED 146,806/yr for Sep 2027, AAB-BBB offer) is roughly 2.4x Murdoch's fee: keep it as an aspirational halo peer in "best for business" search, not a like-for-like price peer.
