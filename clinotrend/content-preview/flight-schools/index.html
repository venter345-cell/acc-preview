Flight schools page, for review

A new page for flight school owners, CFIs and training managers: how group medicals work, a short group enquiry form, a shareable student booking link, and a one-page student handout. Preview only: nothing here is on the live site.

How to review and comment

  1. Open a page on a computer, or use the phone view link to see it at 390 px wide.
  2. To leave a note, open the page, tap the dark Add comment button at the bottom left, tap the exact spot your note is about, type it and tap Save comment. Add your name the first time. Comments also work inside the phone frame.
  3. The group enquiry form is preview only: nothing is sent to aCc. Sending opens a thank you page.

This preview is noindex. The live site, cPanel and the held go-live package have not been changed.

What is on the page

  • The student pathway: a Class 2 medical is needed for an SPL (Part 61 regulation 61.02.1 and Part 67 regulation 67.00.2). Recreational students may need Class 4. Class 1 is not issued here.
  • What students should bring, typical turnaround, and a chest X-ray note with two nearby independent radiology practices (phone ahead).
  • A short Book a group form (school name, contact, mobile, email, rough student count, preferred weeks, Class 2 or Class 4). On the live site this would go to aCc with the tag flight-school-enquiry, with no medical data.
  • Individual booking links, phone 011 568 7979 and WhatsApp 078 255 1405.
  • A shareable student booking link with UTM tags, and a printable one-page handout titled Your SACAA medical.
  • An FAQ for schools, including consent before confirming attendance, and fees from R1,500 only.
