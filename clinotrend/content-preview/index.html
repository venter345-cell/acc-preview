Articles for review: aviation medical guides

8 guides for the ClinoTrend website, written to answer the questions pilots and cabin crew type into Google and AI assistants. They are drafts on a private preview. Nothing here is on the live site.

How to review and comment

  1. Open an article. Read on desktop shows it as it will look on a computer. View as phone shows it at phone size. You can also open this page on your phone.
  2. To leave a note, tap the dark Add comment button (speech bubble) at the bottom left of the article, then tap the exact spot your note is about, type it and tap Save comment. For a note on the whole article, use General comment. Add your name the first time.
  3. Look out for the yellow boxes marked [DOC TO CONFIRM]. These are points we need you to check before the article goes live. Please reply to each one with a comment.
  4. Every article lists its official sources (SACAA and the Civil Aviation Regulations, Part 67) at the bottom.

The yellow boxes and the "Draft" labels only show on this preview. Once you approve an article, we remove them and publish it on clinotrend-health.co.za with your name as the author.
