Growth plan: content calendar and Gauteng aviation medicals

Five blog posts a week (Monday to Friday) and two Google Business Profile posts a week (Tuesday and Thursday) for 12 weeks, plus the initiatives that build bookings. Working titles for review; nothing on this page is published on the live site.

  • 60blog posts
  • 24GBP posts
  • 4drafts ready
  • 12weeks

How it runs

  1. STEP 1

    Research

    Search demand from Keyword Planner (once the Ads account is live), Google suggestions, Search Console queries and patient questions.

  2. STEP 2

    Draft

    Written against Part 67 and SACAA sources, HPCSA safe: no superlatives, guarantees, testimonials or prices we have not confirmed.

  3. STEP 3

    Ruben approves

    Ruben checks each draft; anything clinical goes to Dr Ndhlovu before it is published.

  4. STEP 4

    Publish

    Prerendered page, Article and FAQ schema, sitemap and llms.txt updated, GBP post linked to it.

  5. STEP 5

    Track in GA4

    Book clicks, calls and WhatsApp taps per post, Search Console clicks and GBP insights, reviewed monthly.

Dominate Gauteng aviation medicals

Ten initiatives that work alongside the content plan. Each is factual and HPCSA safe: no inducements, comparisons or testimonials in patient facing copy.

  1. 1

    AI answer visibility

    llms.txt, FAQ schema and clear Q and A pages are live. Each month we ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity which practice they suggest for "aviation medical Gauteng" and similar questions, record the answers and fix the gaps they show.

  2. 2

    Reviews engine

    Every patient gets the same neutral review request after the visit, with HPCSA safe replies to each review. Goal: the largest number of Google reviews among Gauteng aviation medical examiners.

  3. 3

    Renewal reminders (GHL)

    Reminders by WhatsApp or SMS timed to each patient's certificate expiry date, with consent, so renewals are booked well before the deadline. Only front desk details are stored in GHL.

  4. 4

    Flight school and cabin crew academy partnerships

    Referral pipeline with schools at Grand Central, Lanseria, Rand and Wonderboom: intake checklists and group booking days. No commissions, gifts or inducements.

  5. 5

    Citations

    Consistent name, address and phone on the SACAA examiner register, the Grand Central tenant directory, AOPA SA, SA Flyer, Pilot's Post and medical directories.

  6. 6

    Google Ads on high-intent terms

    Search campaigns for terms such as "aviation medical examiner Johannesburg" and "class 2 medical renewal", geo-targeted around the four Gauteng airfields, with GA4 conversion tracking.

  7. 7

    LinkedIn

    Profile refresh for Dr Ndhlovu and a company page for the practice, sharing the guides and practice updates.

  8. 8

    Location pages

    Pages for Midrand, Johannesburg, Pretoria and Centurion with real directions, travel routes and FAQs, linked from the guides.

  9. 9

    Tools that earn links

    The "when does my medical expire" calculator is live in the validity guide. Next: a printable checklist and a renewal reminder sign-up that flight schools can link to.

  10. 10

    Monthly reporting dashboard

    One page each month: bookings, calls, WhatsApp taps, reviews, rankings, AI answer checks and Ads spend against bookings.

Keyword evidence for the first four posts: content/blog/KEYWORDS.md in the site source. Search volumes are directional until Keyword Planner access is live.
